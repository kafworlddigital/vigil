Mac audio and shortcut repair — build 10

Default global listen shortcut: Control+Option+V. It is shown in Settings with
Change, on the face's welcome card, and on the first introduction card. Changes
check system symbolic hotkeys and use exclusive Carbon registration, keeping
the previous key if a candidate cannot be registered. Normal launches no longer
register Option+Space. The new --vigil-audio-check is the current hardware check;
the historical --vigil-nudge-check still contains its old Option+Space test and
must not be used on a machine with competing launchers.

The chosen-colour glow is drawn inside the face Canvas, clipped to the exact
animated BlobShape rather than the panel rectangle. It pulses three times in
2.4 seconds. Real native own-window view captures were visually inspected;
these are not independent WindowServer screenshots. The Settings bitmap is
blank and is not accepted as visual evidence, although its real Sound check
button was successfully pressed through the app's accessibility tree and its
Change control was present.

Voice permissions are queried without prompting at launch, hotkey activation
or Sound check. A single Allow card requests Microphone then Speech Recognition
only after a click. Previously denied access opens the relevant system privacy
pane. Existing grants were both authorised in the installed app; no permission
reset or overnight system prompt was needed. Both Info.plist usage descriptions
and the signed audio-input entitlement were verified. The app is not sandboxed.

Wake recognition checks on-device en-GB, then en-US availability. Contextual
strings include Vigil and Virgil. Continuous transcripts can find a fresh name
after earlier conversation, and input levels/errors are visible. Microphone
route changes restart the listener. No cloud speech fallback is used.

Speech callback buffers are copied before crossing queues, preserving their
contents and order. Chime and rendered speech explicitly select the current
default output UID and apply the app volume. Settings Sound check plays the
chime, says Vigil here, and shows a live microphone meter, route and result.

On this test Mac, the built-in microphone delivered only zeros because the
MacBook lid was confirmed closed. Apple documents the hardware microphone
disconnect for closed Apple silicon MacBook lids. The attached C922 microphone
was selected as default input. Its initial 40% level produced intermittent
synthetic Vigil detection; Virgil passed. A final 65% input-level check passed
both real speaker-to-microphone names and complete what-is-this commands with
healthy, unclipped peaks. The 65% input level was retained. This is synthetic
speech played through real hardware, not a claim Keith personally spoke tonight.

The final installed check also passed real on-device file recognition of both
names in both en-GB and en-US. Permissions were authorised; output was MacBook
Pro Speakers at the existing 58.39%, unmuted, and app volume 50%. Both players
remained isPlaying while their playheads advanced and meters showed signal;
the default output device was running. This uses the explicitly requested
player/device alternative to loopback, not an independently recorded audible
waveform. No headphone route or future device switch was physically tested.

47 portable core tests passed on Contabo, plus macOS Swift syntax and edition
isolation. Native Mac Release succeeded on the sole permitted retry after a
glow-variable naming clash in attempt one. No local iOS/simulator build ran.
The previous build 9 was backed up to a verified ZIP; /Applications/Vigil.app
is the sole Keith app bundle in audited internal roots. Its exact Dock pin is
retained and remaining pins keep their relative order. A separate Sermo Voice
pin disappeared during this interval; no Dock write was performed by this task,
and the unrelated change was left intact. Dedicated dd-tonight was deleted. Vigil was
ordinarily relaunched without proof arguments. No Git push was requested/run.
